About MotoDoug

MotoDoug Designs is born from the trail. Every mount, bracket, and accessory is designed, printed, and tested on real adventure bikes in real conditions.

What started as a personal hobby of solving problems on my own rides has grown into a small shop creating functional, durable 3D-printed gear for riders who push their machines beyond the pavement.

Whether it’s a custom GPS mount, light bar, or tank accessory, every piece is made with the same care I put into my own CFMoto Ibex 450 (MT) setup.
